North East Hampshire Conservative Association
Annual Association Dinner
Wednesday 2 December 2026 · The Elvetham, Hook

Dear Members and friends,

Get ready for our Annual Association Dinner. We are delighted to invite you to The Elvetham on Wednesday 2 December 2026 for an evening of good food, good company and a speaker we are very proud to welcome.

Our guest speaker is Robert Colvile, Head of Small Business and Entrepreneurship at the Centre for Policy Studies, and a columnist for The Sunday Times. He directed the Centre for Policy Studies from 2017 until September 2026, and was Editor-in-Chief of CapX. He co-authored the 2019 Conservative manifesto and wrote The Great Acceleration.

The evening begins with a sparkling wine reception on arrival in The Library, followed by a three course dinner, with soft drinks and water served throughout the evening. There will be a raffle, and our silent auction star lot is the chance to bid for a chauffeured Bentley Mulsanne for lunch or dinner, the winner’s choice, with access to the champagne fridge, for up to three people in the car, subject to a radius of 35 miles from Hook.

Date: Wednesday 2 December 2026
Venue: The Library, The Elvetham, Hook
