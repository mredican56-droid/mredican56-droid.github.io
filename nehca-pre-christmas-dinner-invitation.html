Guest Speaker

Robert Colvile

One of the most influential voices in Conservative thinking, Robert Colvile is Head of Small Business and Entrepreneurship at the Centre for Policy Studies — the think tank founded by Margaret Thatcher and Sir Keith Joseph — having stepped down in September 2026 after nine years as its Director. He writes a weekly column for The Sunday Times.

In December 2019 he stepped away from the CPS to co-author the Conservative Party’s general election manifesto. Before that he was Head of Comment at The Daily and Sunday Telegraph and News Director of BuzzFeed UK. His critically acclaimed book The Great Acceleration: How the World Is Getting Faster, Faster (Bloomsbury, 2016) examined how technology is speeding up every part of modern life.

  • Head of Small Business and Entrepreneurship, Centre for Policy Studies; Director of the CPS (2017 to 2026) and Editor-in-Chief of CapX
  • Co-author of the 2019 Conservative Party general election manifesto
  • Columnist, The Sunday Times; formerly Head of Comment at the Telegraph
  • Adviser to the JCB Group; advisory board member, PricedOut
